Udemy certificate check

One-time setup: drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

Check Udemy certificate

Each time:

  1. Log in to this platform as faculty in this browser.
  2. Open the certificate's Udemy page (ude.my/UC-… or udemy.com/certificate/UC-…) in your own browser.
  3. Click the bookmark. This page opens and compares Udemy's recipient name with the uploaded certificate.

Udemy blocks automated requests, but it shows the page normally to you, so your browser does the lookup and this platform does the comparison.
